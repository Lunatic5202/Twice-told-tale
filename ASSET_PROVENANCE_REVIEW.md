# Asset provenance review

This is a release-preparation checklist, not a grant of rights or a legal opinion. It records what the repository currently documents and what still needs primary-source evidence.

## Current findings

| Asset group | What the repository says | Evidence gap / action |
|---|---|---|
| Backgrounds and CG plates | Generated by `tools/make_art.py` | Keep the generator and generated outputs together; confirm no externally sourced textures or source images are introduced later. |
| Character sprites | Sliced by `tools/import_sheets.py` from hand-supplied expression sheets | The importer references local Downloads paths that are not present in the repository. Record each sheet's creator, source, date obtained, license/permission, and whether redistribution and modification are allowed. |
| Music | Listed as CC0 in `game/audio/CREDITS.md` and fetched by `tools/fetch_music.sh` | Credits currently list track names and OpenGameArt.org, but not individual asset-page URLs, uploader identities, or archived license statements. Verify every source before redistribution. |
| Sound effects | Listed as CC0 in `game/audio/CREDITS.md` and fetched by `tools/fetch_sfx.sh` | Add the individual asset-page URLs, pack author/uploader, exact license evidence, and the date checked. |
| Cinzel Decorative and Crimson Text | Identified as SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `ASSETS.md` | Include or link the authoritative OFL license text and record the font source/version. |
| Hassengard | Identified as freeware, non-commercial in `ASSETS.md` | Preserve the original license/readme and verify that the intended fan-game distribution method is permitted. Do not assume it can be used in a commercial release. |
| Genshin Impact characters and setting | Fan work; README disclaims affiliation | Franchise rights remain with their respective owners. The repository's own license does not grant rights to franchise material. |

## Release gates

Before publishing or redistributing a build:

1. Resolve the origin and permission for every hand-supplied sprite sheet.
2. Verify each music and SFX source against its original asset page; archive the page URL, creator, license text/identifier, and retrieval date.
3. Preserve the required font license files and any notices.
4. Confirm the Hassengard non-commercial terms are compatible with the planned distribution.
5. Keep the fan-work and no-affiliation statement visible.
6. Re-run this review whenever an asset is replaced or added.

## Important distinction

A file being present in the repository, downloaded by a script, or described as CC0 in an existing credits file does not by itself prove that the project has permission to redistribute it. This checklist intentionally leaves unresolved items unresolved until primary-source evidence is recorded.
